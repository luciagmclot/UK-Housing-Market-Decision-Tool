A Statistical Framework

An empirical guide to buying property across the United Kingdom.

Using a quartile-based distribution across 108 counties and an amortisation model for affordability, this tool classifies any listing against its local market and the buyer's financial capacity.

Abstract We combine county-level price percentiles with a standard mortgage amortisation model to produce a composite score bounded in [0, 100]. Decision thresholds follow FCA affordability guidance (30%/40% of gross income) and quartile-based deviation from local medians.
