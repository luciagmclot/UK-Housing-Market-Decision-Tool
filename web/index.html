A Statistical Framework

An empirical guide to buying property across the United Kingdom.

A V4 housing-decision model: the listed price is positioned against county-level quartile statistics, the loan and affordability are derived from a standard amortisation, and the 5-year outlook is simulated using Geometric Brownian Motion calibrated to the local historical log-return and volatility.

Abstract The composite score combines (i) price fairness against county quartiles, (ii) payment-to-income ratio under FCA thresholds, (iii) loan-to-value, (iv) historical CAGR, (v) volatility, and (vi) the simulated probability of price appreciation over 5 years. Score is bounded in [0, 100] and mapped to a four-level recommendation.
